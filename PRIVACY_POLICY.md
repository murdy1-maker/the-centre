# Privacy Policy for The Centre

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Application:** The Centre (Mobile Application for Android)

### 1. Introduction
The Centre ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when using The Centre mobile application.

### 2. Information We Collect
- **User Queries**: Text queries or topics submitted by users to request an analysis synthesis.
- **Source Material**: Text excerpts or article links provided by users for perspective deconstruction.
- **Technical & Device Information**: Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics (e.g. app version, error crash logs) used strictly to maintain app performance.
- **No Personal Identifiers**: The Centre does not require account creation, name, email address, phone number, location, or biometric data to use core features.

### 3. How Information Is Processed
- User queries and submitted text are processed through our AI analysis pipeline strictly to extract structural claims, map conflicts, and deliver the central synthesis.
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize your search queries or personal data.
- Data is not used to build individual user tracking profiles.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The App interacts with our cloud API and AI processing backends (e.g. Google Gemini API) to perform analysis. These third-party services process queries in compliance with enterprise data protection standards.

### 5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit between the mobile application and the backend service.

### 6. Children's Privacy
The Centre is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.
